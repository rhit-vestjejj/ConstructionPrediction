How accurate are the forecasts?

Every model was tested on years it never saw: trained on earlier years, then scored on later ones. These tables show how it did.

How to read the numbers

PR-AUC (0 to 1, higher is better): how well the model puts the counties that really surged at the top of its list. Random guessing scores about the base rate, the share of counties that surge in a typical year.

Precision@100 / top 50: of the counties the model rated highest, the share that actually surged or got a project.

Baseline rows are simple rules of thumb (e.g. "big counties stay big"). A model is only useful if it beats them.
