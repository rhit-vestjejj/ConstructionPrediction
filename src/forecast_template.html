Predictions are for construction activity proxies (permits and construction jobs) at county level, from models evaluated on held-out later years. They are conditional model estimates, not guarantees or causal claims.

Forecast settings

H3 cells are only used where dated, located permits exist (category "Metro permits"). Elsewhere models predict at county level, and smaller cells would imply precision that doesn't exist.
