Where is US construction happening?

County construction employment (BLS QCEW) with OpenStreetMap data center sites. Hover a county for details.

Jobs are private-sector annual average construction employment (NAICS 23/236/237/238), a proxy for activity rather than dollars of work or permits. Data center sites are from OpenStreetMap: current locations only, no build dates, and coverage is uneven. QCEW 2025 is still preliminary and omitted.
